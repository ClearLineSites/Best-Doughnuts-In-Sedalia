Our story

A friendly local favorite

Best Doughnuts is the kind of shop people in Sedalia send their out-of-town guests to. We keep the cases full of glazed, chocolate, sprinkles, and specialty doughnuts, pour a reliable cup of coffee, and round out the morning with breakfast sandwiches, kolaches, muffins, and biscuits.

Stop in for a dozen to share, a quiet coffee, or a quick breakfast on the way through town. Follow us on Facebook for the day's specials, fresh photos, and any hour updates.
